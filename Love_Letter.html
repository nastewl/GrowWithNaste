Dear My Half-soul, Ella

Privet, How are you? I hope you’re doing well.
Actually, there are so many things I’d like to share with you,
but thank you for everything You were truly amazing today You look so beautiful
May God always protect you take your health
im lucky have you

Pushkin whistles in his poetry.

I love you in silence, without hope; at times tormented by shyness, at other times by jealousy. I love you sincerely and tenderly—may God grant that you be loved by another man.

Your Love, Nastenka
